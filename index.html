My Love

A little piece of my heart, written just for you

My love,

I don't know if words will ever be enough to explain how much you mean to me, but I want to try.

You are not just someone I love. You are someone I can see in my future. When I think about the life I want to build, the person I want beside me is you.

I want you to know that my feelings for you are serious. I don't want you to be just a chapter in my life. I want you to be part of the whole story.

I want us to grow together, laugh together, face difficult days together and celebrate the beautiful ones together.

I promise that I will always try to communicate with you, listen to you, respect you and support you. I won't promise that everything will always be perfect, but I promise that when things become difficult, I will choose to work through them with you.

I want you to become more than my girlfriend. I want you to become my fiancée, my wife, my best friend, and my partner for life.

I want us to build something real. Something strong enough to survive distance, challenges, misunderstandings and the difficult seasons of life.

And if you ever wonder how serious I am about you, remember this: I choose you.

I want you today.
I want you tomorrow.
I want you for the rest of my life. ❤️

I don't know exactly what the future will look like, but I know who I want beside me when I get there.

You.

So this isn't just a love letter. It's my promise that my intentions with you are real.

I hope that one day, when we look back at where everything started, we will smile and say, "This was the beginning of forever."

Will you be my forever? ❤️
With all my heart,
Your Love ❤️
